# Shadow Clone

**An AI meeting assistant that answers in your voice using context you’ve approved.**

When two meetings overlap, a teammate may need a project update or a decision that only you can give. Shadow Clone can handle routine questions using context you’ve confirmed and bring you in when a reply needs your judgment.

## How it works

1. **Give it context.** Create a profile, confirm the memories you want it to use, and enroll a voice sample you can revoke.
2. **Start a live session.** Share a Google Meet tab in Chrome and enable the assistant. It announces itself as AI before listening and includes a spoken disclosure in each reply.
3. **Stay in control.** The assistant uses your confirmed context to answer routine questions. When it flags a reply for approval, it withholds the reply and asks for your input.

## What you can try

Start with a text simulation to review and approve proposed answers. The live voice workflow uses Chrome tab audio and a manually configured virtual audio cable. Live Gnani provider calls still need verification.
